Senior software engineering manager, Ireland. Backend engineer turned people leader.

About

I'm a senior software engineering manager at Expel, a managed detection and response company, where I built and lead a team in the Workbench Platform organization. We run an internal platform that lets developers build event-driven, serverless solutions on AWS. My job is delivery, platform reliability and helping the people on my team grow.

We build the integrations that sync status from Expel Workbench to customers' security devices, including CrowdStrike Falcon, SentinelOne and Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, and I'm accountable for Workbench's notification system, which delivers alerts through email, Slack and Microsoft Teams.

I started out in high-performance computing and systems work, then spent my early career as a hands-on backend engineer at Fidelity Investments and Workday, writing Python and Ruby on AWS, GCP and Kubernetes: cloud provisioning APIs, developer tooling, and REST and GraphQL services. Over time I moved from writing the code to leading the teams that write it, with a focus on technical leadership, Agile coaching and career development. I studied at the University of Galway.

Things I'm proud of

  • Delivering Expel's AWS serverless platform and migrating legacy systems onto it.
  • Building an AI-driven operations solution with Anthropic's Claude that automatically tracks, enriches and triages operational alerts. It cuts operational toil so the team can focus on project-led work, and it surfaces patterns of issues that help us improve reliability and resilience.
  • Hiring the team. I wrote the job descriptions and ads, designed the interview plan, and created the onboarding plans for the people who joined.
  • Establishing the first engineering on-call rotation in Ireland.
  • Running an organisation-wide career development programme at Workday, giving individual contributors short-term “gigs” to build new skills and experience.

What I enjoy

I enjoy understanding systems and problems, pulling threads and asking questions until I have the whole picture, and then owning the solution. I also like finding the right experiences to help people develop their careers, and working with Product Management to turn customer needs into better products.
