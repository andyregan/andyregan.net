02 / About

I'm a senior software engineering manager at Expel, a managed detection and response company, where I built and lead a team of six engineers in the Workbench Platform organization. We run an internal platform that lets developers build event-driven, serverless solutions on AWS. My job is delivery, platform reliability and helping the people on my team grow.

I started out in high-performance computing and systems work, then spent my early career as a hands-on backend engineer at Fidelity Investments and Workday, writing Python and Ruby on AWS, GCP and Kubernetes: cloud provisioning APIs, developer tooling, and REST and GraphQL services. Over time I moved from writing the code to leading the teams that write it, with a focus on technical leadership, Agile coaching and career development. I took my first management role at Workday in 2021, leading a four-person team on a reliability initiative before a six-person cross-functional team, and I spent a period as acting Product Owner, building roadmaps and leading prioritisation. I studied at the University of Galway.
